The calm little workflow

Tell Muse. Muse records. Muse checks. Muse alerts.

The product stays intentionally narrow: your list, official notices, and a focused message only when the evidence lines up.

1

Tell Muse what you bought

Say the product, brand, date, store, and lot or best-by code when available. Muse records only what you say.

2

Tell Muse what you are buying

Planned shopping stays planned until you confirm the purchase. A pre-shopping check is time-bound and clearly labeled.

3

Keep regular brands

Brands you explicitly buy regularly can stay eligible for checks even when no new purchase was reported.

4

Muse checks

On its configured schedule, Muse checks new official FDA openFDA Food Enforcement and USDA FSIS notices.

5

Muse matches

Exact product and brand evidence comes first. Lot, UPC, and date details refine the match. Similar names do not count.

6

Muse alerts

A match includes the reason, recall class when provided, what to do, and the official notice link. No match means silence.

Important: Muse is an informational matching layer, not FDA, USDA, a doctor, or a guarantee that every food is safe.
